## Term Project: AI Study Assistant

**Course:** CIS 641 System Analysis and Design &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Role:** sole analyst, designer, and developer

**The problem.** Students keep their school work in many places: due dates in the learning system, study plans in a calendar, and notes in folders. Because these tools do not talk to each other, a student can see that an exam is coming but not that they have barely studied for it.

**What I built.** The AI Study Assistant is a web application that puts courses, assignments, exams, deadlines, study schedules, and course materials in one place, and tracks how many hours the student studies for each course. Its AI feature compares each upcoming deadline with the student's study time and recommends what to study next, with a suggested plan of study sessions. The student can accept the plan, which adds the sessions to their schedule, or dismiss it.

**How I did it.** This semester I took the project through the full analysis and design process:

- Wrote the project proposal, scope, and feasibility study
- Gathered and organized the functional and non-functional requirements into a Software Requirements Specification
- Modeled the system with a UML class diagram, CRC cards, a sequence diagram, and a state machine diagram
- Designed the system on the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js) with Docker, and a recommendation engine where rules calculate the study plan and a pretrained language model writes the explanation

**What it shows.** Requirements analysis, object-oriented design, full-stack web development, and responsible use of AI, where the model explains a decision but does not make up the numbers.
