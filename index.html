04 · Term Project

AI Study Assistant

CIS 641 System Analysis and Design, Fall 2026 · Sole analyst, designer, and developer

The problem. Students keep their work in many places: due dates in the learning system, study plans in a calendar, notes in folders. Because these tools don't talk to each other, a student can see that an exam is coming but not that they have barely studied for it.

What I'm building. A web application that puts courses, assignments, exams, deadlines, study schedules, and course materials in one place, and tracks study hours per course. Its AI feature compares each upcoming deadline with the student's study time and recommends what to study next, with a plan of study sessions the student can accept or dismiss.

Design choice I'm proud of. Rules calculate the study gap and the plan; a pretrained language model only writes the explanation. The model can't invent hours or dates, so the advice stays trustworthy, the same idea that drives my XAI research.

  2. 02Requirements gathered into a Software Requirements Specification
  3. 03UML class diagram, CRC cards, sequence and state machine diagrams
  4. 04MERN stack (MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js) with Docker
